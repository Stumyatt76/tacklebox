Private by design

Your swim stays yours.

Tacklebox has no backend and no analytics. The only data that ever leaves your phone goes to the public weather, river and tide services when you ask for local conditions.

  • No account, no login, no social feedOpen the app and start logging. There's nothing to sign up for.
  • Your precise fishing spots are never storedLocation is requested at reduced accuracy and used only to fetch conditions.
  • Your journal lives on your deviceOn iPhone it can sync through your own private iCloud. We run no server and cannot see it.
  • Take it with youExport JSON records, a checksummed photo backup, or a Season Book PDF whenever you like.
